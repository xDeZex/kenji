# Toyota Kata's second coach — an observer for the coach, never the learner

**Situation**: In a coaching structure, an outside observer role can either advise the coach on how they're coaching, or weigh in with the person being coached directly — these are different roles with different effects, and conflating them contaminates the coach-learner exchange with a third voice.

**Practice**: Toyota Kata's own practice defines a "second coach" — an observer whose job is to watch the coach-learner exchange and give the coach feedback on their coaching technique. The second coach never addresses the learner directly. This keeps the primary exchange clean while still giving the coach a check on their own practice.

**Source**: Gemba Academy, "What Is the Role of the Second Coach in Kata?" (Toyota Kata coaching-kata practice literature, Lean Enterprise Institute / Gemba Academy).
